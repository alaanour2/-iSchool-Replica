Privacy Policy

1. What we collect

We collect the details you enter when you register or contact us: name, email address, phone number and the age or grade of the student. Payment details are handled by the payment provider and are not stored on this website.

2. How we use it

Your details are used to create your account, schedule classes, match a suitable instructor, send class updates and respond to support requests.

3. Storage

This demo website keeps accounts created through the sign-up form in your own browser storage only. They are not uploaded to a server and are removed when you clear your browser data.

4. Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Information is shared only with the instructor assigned to your child and, where required, with service providers that help us run classes and payments.

5. Children's privacy

Accounts for children are managed by a parent or guardian. We ask that only the minimum information needed for teaching is provided.

6. Your choices

You can ask us to review, correct or delete your account details at any time by emailing Support@ischooltech.com.
